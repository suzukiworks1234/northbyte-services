Northbyte Services provides project-based technical services for businesses and individuals, including custom scripts, spreadsheet and CSV processing, data cleanup, testing, and workflow automation. Work is delivered remotely as digital files, source code, documentation, or other agreed project outputs.

Services

CSV and Excel transformation, validation, deduplication, cleanup, filtering, reporting, and repeatable processing workflows.

Software automation

Small software utilities, Python-based automation, command-line tools, and task-specific scripts designed around an agreed specification.

Quality & reliability

Automated tests, reproducible examples, documentation, and verification of outputs so that delivered work can be reviewed and reused.

How projects are delivered

1. Scope. Requirements, deliverables, acceptance criteria, timing, and price are agreed before work begins.
2. Build. Work is completed remotely using the agreed inputs and specifications.
3. Verify. Deliverables are checked against the agreed requirements and, where appropriate, include tests or sample outputs.
4. Deliver. Digital deliverables are provided through the project marketplace or communication channel used for the engagement.

Pricing is project-based and depends on scope and complexity. Northbyte Services may accept engagements through online marketplaces such as RenX, where project terms and payments are handled through the applicable marketplace.

Customer support

Support for an active project is provided through the same marketplace or project communication channel used for the engagement. Questions about delivered work, corrections within the agreed scope, and delivery status should be sent through that project channel so the request can be matched to the correct engagement.
