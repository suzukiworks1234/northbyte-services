Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

Northbyte Services provides project-based digital services. This website does not use advertising trackers, analytics cookies, or account registration.

Project information

When a customer engages Northbyte Services through a third-party marketplace or project channel, information needed to perform the agreed work may be received through that platform. Such information is used only to communicate about, perform, verify, and deliver the relevant project, unless another use is required by law.

Data minimization

Customers should avoid providing personal, confidential, regulated, or sensitive data unless it is necessary for the agreed project and the handling requirements have been agreed in advance.

Retention

Project materials are retained only as reasonably necessary to complete the work, support the delivered result, meet marketplace requirements, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Third-party platforms

Projects may be arranged and paid for through third-party marketplaces. Those platforms process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

Contact

For privacy questions relating to an active engagement, please use the marketplace or project communication channel associated with that engagement.
