Service Terms

Scope of work

Each project is governed by the scope, deliverables, acceptance criteria, timing, and price agreed for that engagement. Where a project is arranged through a third-party marketplace, the marketplace terms and the project listing or agreement also apply.

Delivery

Services are delivered remotely. Deliverables may include source code, scripts, processed data files, documentation, tests, reports, or other digital outputs agreed for the project.

Customer responsibilities

The customer is responsible for providing lawful access to any data, files, systems, or materials required for the project and for confirming that submitted materials may be used for the agreed purpose.

Changes and corrections

Corrections needed to meet the agreed acceptance criteria will be handled through the project communication channel. Requests that materially change the agreed scope may require a revised project agreement.

Payment and cancellations

When a project is purchased through a third-party marketplace, payment, cancellation, refund, and dispute handling follow that marketplace's applicable terms and project agreement.

No affiliation

Northbyte Services is an independent service provider and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any third-party marketplace mentioned on this website.

Contact

For questions about an active project, please use the marketplace or project communication channel associated with that engagement.
